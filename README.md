# 📊 Employee Attrition & Workforce Insights Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyses workforce data to uncover why employees leave and where HR should focus retention efforts.

![HR Analytics Dashboard](HR_Dashboard.png)

## 🎯 Business Questions Answered

- What is the overall attrition rate, and how has it changed?
- Which departments lose the most employees?
- How do gender and marital status relate to attrition?
- What does the workforce look like by age, role and tenure?

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Overall attrition rate is **X%** across **N** employees
- **[Department]** has the highest attrition at **X%**
- Employees in **[group]** are most likely to leave
- **[Another finding]**

## 📈 Dashboard Features

- **6 KPI cards:** headcount, attrition count, attrition rate, and more
- **6 interactive charts:** department, gender, demographics and attrition trends
- **Slicers:** Gender, Marital Status, Department
